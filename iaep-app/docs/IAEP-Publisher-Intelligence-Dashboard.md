# IAEP Publisher & Executive Intelligence Dashboard (v1.1 Additive)

* **Version**: 1.1 (Additive Architecture Blueprint)
* **Status**: APPROVED FOR DESIGN
* **Domain**: Executive Analytics

---

## 1. Konsolidasi Data Agregat (PT Bernas Sumut Jaya)
Dasbor agregat menyajikan data statistik dari seluruh jurnal ilmiah di bawah naungan penerbit:

* **Agregasi Utama:** Total Journals, Total Articles, Total Published Articles.
* **Cakupan Pengenal:** Total ORCID Coverage (Editor/Reviewer), Total DOI Registered, Total Zenodo Deposits.
* **Analitik Dampak:** Total Citations, Total Views, Total Downloads.

---

## 2. KPI Strategis Eksekutif (Executive Radar)
- **Publication Growth Trend:** Visualisasi laju publikasi per semester.
- **Acceptance Rate & Turnaround Time:** Rata-rata waktu peer-review naskah hingga desk decision.
- **SDG Mapping:** Klasifikasi artikel berdasarkan tujuan pembangunan berkelanjutan PBB (Sustainable Development Goals).

Seluruh data disajikan secara **read-only** tanpa hak akses untuk memanipulasi daur hidup naskah v1.0.
